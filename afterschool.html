After-School Programs

Providing a safe and nurturing environment for girls to learn and grow beyond school hours.

About the Program

Our After-School Programs offer a variety of activities that encourage learning, creativity, and personal development. We provide a safe space where girls can explore their interests and build new skills.

Program Activities:

  • Homework assistance and tutoring
  • Art, music, and sports clubs
  • Workshops on self-expression and creativity
