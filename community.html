About the Program

Our Community Outreach initiatives aim to engage with local communities and promote the value of girls' education. We work closely with families and leaders to break down barriers to education.

Initiative Highlights:

  • Community workshops and seminars
  • Parent and guardian engagement programs
  • Public awareness campaigns
