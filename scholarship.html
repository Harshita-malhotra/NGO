Scholarship Program

Providing financial support for girls from low-income families to pursue their education.

About the Program

The Scholarship Program aims to eliminate financial barriers to education by providing scholarships to deserving girls from underprivileged backgrounds. Our goal is to ensure that every girl has the opportunity to achieve her educational dreams.

Key Benefits:

  • Full or partial tuition coverage
  • Monthly stipends for educational expenses
  • Mentoring and academic support
