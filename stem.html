STEM Workshops

Encouraging girls to explore science, technology, engineering, and math through engaging workshops.

About the Program

Our STEM Workshops are designed to ignite interest in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics among girls. Through hands-on activities and real-world projects, we aim to foster curiosity and critical thinking.

Workshop Topics:

  • Basic coding and programming
  • Robotics and engineering challenges
  • Science experiments and innovations
