Mentorship Program

Connecting girls with inspiring female mentors to guide their educational journey.

About the Program

Our Mentorship Program pairs young girls with accomplished female professionals who serve as role models and advisors. Through regular mentorship sessions, we aim to build confidence, foster personal growth, and inspire academic success.

Program Highlights:

  • One-on-one mentorship sessions
  • Career guidance and counseling
  • Workshops and leadership training
